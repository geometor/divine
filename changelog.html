0.4.0¶

2026-11-12

added

  • Catalog of Golden Ratio Constructions (geometor.divine.catalog): 45 100% exact symbolic SymPy / geometor.model constructions across 7 geometric families (GC-01.01 through GC-07.06), cataloging classical propositions, Hofstetter / Odom / Bui Quang Tuan / Tran Quang Hung constructions, Sangaku circle packings, and dissection paradoxes curated by Alexander Bogomolny (Cut-the-Knot) and peer-reviewed journals (Forum Geometricorum, Crux Mathematicorum, Mathematical Reflections, AMM).

  • Exact Rational Trigonometry quadrance certificates (GoldenCertificate), Lemoine Geometrographie step counts, automated golden-section and golden-chain discovery (129 sections, 33 chains across the catalog), interactive .euclid-graphic SVG renderer, and multi-track Book Codex under docsrc/constructions/.

  • Added find_chains_in_model() and implicit collinear triple screening to geometor.divine.golden.

fixed

  • Fixed an order-dependent in_chain pruning bug in find_chains() and upgraded golden-section scanning to combine fast numerical pre-filtering with exact Rational Trigonometry quadrance and zero_test verification.
