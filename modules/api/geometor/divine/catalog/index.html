geometor.divine.catalog¶

Exact Symbolic Catalog of Golden Ratio Constructions & Theorems.

Catalogs 45 geometric constructions and theorems across 7 families—curated from classical antiquity, Forum Geometricorum, Mathematics Magazine, American Mathematical Monthly, and Alexander Bogomolny’s Cut-the-Knot archive—verified with 100% exact SymPy symbolic algebra, Rational Trigonometry quadrances, and automated geometor.divine golden-chain discovery.
