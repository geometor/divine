geometor.divine.catalog.pentagons_polygons¶

Family 05: Regular Pentagons, Pentagrams, Irregular Pentagons & Polygonal Stars.

Catalogs Claudius Ptolemy’s Almagest pentagon and pentagram, Dao Thanh Oai’s regular pentagon perpendicular, V. V. Prasolov & Bùi Quang Tuân’s equi-area irregular pentagon, and Tran Quang Hung’s pentagon-hexagon, six-pentagon star, and fivefold Wu Xing equilateral star theorems.
