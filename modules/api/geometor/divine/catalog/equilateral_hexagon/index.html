geometor.divine.catalog.equilateral_hexagon¶

Family 02: Equilateral Triangles & Regular Hexagons — The Odom Lineage.

Catalogs George Odom’s celebrated 1983 equilateral midline construction and six modern descendants by Michel Bataille, Bùi Quang Tuân, Tran Quang Hung, and Nguyen Thanh Dung.
