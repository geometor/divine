geometor.divine.catalog.minimal_compass¶

Family 01: Classical & Minimal-Step Compass-and-Straightedge Constructions.

Catalogs Euclid’s foundational Elements II.11 division, the right-triangle hypotenuse transfer, Émile Lemoine and Kurt Hofstetter’s minimal 5-step constructions, Mascheroni 5-circle compass-only division, rusty-compass division, and Bùi Quang Tuân’s 5-step circle secant construction.
