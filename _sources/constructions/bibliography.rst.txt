Bibliography
============

.. rubric:: Primary Treatises, Journal Articles & Cut-the-Knot Archival Sources

Primary mathematical treatises, peer-reviewed journal articles, and curated archival pages from :person:`Alexander Bogomolny <alexander-bogomolny>`'s *Interactive Mathematics Miscellany and Puzzles* (*Cut-the-Knot*, © 1996–2018).

.. _cite-euclid-elements:

**Elements (Heath Edition)** (c. 300 BCE)
   *Euclid* — Book II Prop. 11; Book IV Prop. 10–11; Book VI Def. 3 & Prop. 30; Book XIII Prop. 1–10. Sir Thomas L. Heath, The Thirteen Books of Euclid's Elements, Cambridge University Press, 1908 (GEOMETOR Digital Scriptorium edition).
   URL: `https://geometor.github.io/euclid/ <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/>`_
   *Cataloged in*: :doc:`GC-01.01 <01-minimal-compass/01-euclid-ii-11/index>`, :doc:`GC-05.01 <05-pentagons-polygons/01-ptolemy-pentagon-pentagram/index>`

.. _cite-ptolemy-almagest:

**Syntaxis Mathematica (Almagest)** (c. 150 CE)
   *Claudius Ptolemy* — Book I, Chapter 10. G. J. Toomer (trans.), Ptolemy's Almagest, Duckworth / Springer, 1984.
   *Cataloged in*: :doc:`GC-05.01 <05-pentagons-polygons/01-ptolemy-pentagon-pentagram/index>`

.. _cite-kepler-1597:

**Letter to Michael Mästlin (Tübingen, October 1597)** (1597)
   *Johannes Kepler* — Gesammelte Werke, Vol. 13, Nr. 75. First explicit derivation of the right triangle with sides in geometric progression 1 : √φ : φ.
   *Cataloged in*: :doc:`GC-04.02 <04-right-and-345/02-kepler-geometric-triangle/index>`

.. _cite-hooper-1794:

**Rational Recreations** (1794)
   *William Hooper* — 4th ed., Vol. 1, Problem XLIII. Early geometric missing-area rectangle dissection later generalized by Sam Loyd, Martin Gardner, and Paul Curry.
   *Cataloged in*: :doc:`GC-07.06 <07-rhombi-trapezoids-algebra/06-curry-hooper-dissection/index>`

.. _cite-lemoine-1902:

**Géométrographie ou Art des constructions géométriques** (1902)
   *Émile Lemoine* — Scientia No. 18, C. Naud, Paris. Foundational treatise quantifying operational simplicity of straightedge-and-compass constructions; see also J. Reusch, Planimetrische Konstruktionen in geometrographischer Ausführung (1904).
   *Cataloged in*: :doc:`GC-01.03 <01-minimal-compass/03-lemoine-hofstetter-5step/index>`

.. _cite-huntley-1970:

**The Divine Proportion: A Study in Mathematical Beauty** (1970)
   *H. E. Huntley* — Dover Publications, New York. Classic exposition including the angle-bisector circle division in the 3-4-5 right triangle.
   *Cataloged in*: :doc:`GC-01.01 <01-minimal-compass/01-euclid-ii-11/index>`, :doc:`GC-01.02 <01-minimal-compass/02-pythagorean-transfer/index>`, :doc:`GC-04.01 <04-right-and-345/01-huntley-egyptian-345/index>`

.. _cite-odom-coxeter-1983:

**Elementary Problem E3007** (1983)
   *George Odom & H. S. M. Coxeter* — American Mathematical Monthly 90(7), p. 482 (solution 93(7), 1986, p. 566). Equilateral-triangle midline extended to the circumcircle yields the golden section.
   *Cataloged in*: :doc:`GC-02.01 <02-equilateral-hexagon/01-odom-equilateral/index>`

.. _cite-hofstetter-2002:

**A Simple Construction of the Golden Section** (2002)
   *Kurt Hofstetter* — Forum Geometricorum 2, pp. 65–66. Five-circle Mascheroni compass-only construction of the golden section.
   *Cataloged in*: :doc:`GC-01.05 <01-minimal-compass/05-hofstetter-compass-only/index>`

.. _cite-hofstetter-2003:

**5-Step Division of a Segment in the Golden Section** (2003)
   *Kurt Hofstetter* — Forum Geometricorum 3, pp. 205–206. Minimal 5-step straightedge-and-compass internal division of a given segment.
   *Cataloged in*: :doc:`GC-01.03 <01-minimal-compass/03-lemoine-hofstetter-5step/index>`

.. _cite-hofstetter-2004:

**Another 5-Step Division of a Segment in the Golden Section** (2004)
   *Kurt Hofstetter* — Forum Geometricorum 4, pp. 21–22. Alternative 5-step straightedge-and-compass division using three circles and two lines.
   *Cataloged in*: :doc:`GC-01.04 <01-minimal-compass/04-hofstetter-5step-2004/index>`

.. _cite-hofstetter-2005:

**Division of a Segment in the Golden Section with Ruler and Rusty Compass** (2005)
   *Kurt Hofstetter* — Forum Geometricorum 5, pp. 135–136. Fixed-opening (rusty) compass construction dividing a unit segment in φ.
   *Cataloged in*: :doc:`GC-01.06 <01-minimal-compass/06-hofstetter-rusty-compass/index>`

.. _cite-prasolov-2006:

**Problems in Plane Geometry** (2006)
   *V. V. Prasolov* — MCCME, Moscow (5th ed.). Affine equi-area characterization of golden diagonal ratios in irregular convex pentagons.
   *Cataloged in*: :doc:`GC-05.03 <05-pentagons-polygons/03-prasolov-bui-irregular-pentagon/index>`

.. _cite-kocik-2010:

**Golden Window** (2010)
   *Jerzy Kocik* — Mathematics Magazine 83(5), pp. 384–390. Six-circle Apollonian window packing forcing powers of φ, golden rectangles, and Kepler's triangle.
   *Cataloged in*: :doc:`GC-06.05 <06-circles-sangaku/05-kocik-golden-window/index>`

.. _cite-bataille-2011:

**Another Simple Construction of the Golden Section** (2011)
   *Michel Bataille* — Forum Geometricorum 11, pp. 55–56. Constructs φ from an equilateral triangle and square erected on the same segment.
   *Cataloged in*: :doc:`GC-02.02 <02-equilateral-hexagon/02-bataille-triangle-square/index>`

.. _cite-knott-golden-section:

**Fibonacci Numbers and the Golden Section** (1996–2024)
   *Ron Knott* — University of Surrey Mathematical Archive. Companion archive cataloged in sibling GEOMETOR repository geometor/knott.
   URL: `https://r-knott.surrey.ac.uk/Fibonacci/ <https://r-knott.surrey.ac.uk/Fibonacci/>`_
