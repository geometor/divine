GC-07.05 · Tran Quang Hung's Mixtilinear Circle in an Equilateral Triangle
==========================================================================

**Discoverer(s)**: :person:`Tran Quang Hung <tran-quang-hung>`, :person:`Alexander Bogomolny <alexander-bogomolny>`, :person:`Leonard Giugiuc <leo-giugiuc>` | **Date**: 2016 | **Field**: :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \phi)` | **Loci**: 3 (:term:`Lemoine <geometrographie>` :math:`S = 9`)

.. enunciation::

   In equilateral triangle :g:`ABC` with circumcenter :g:`K` and circumcircle :g:`cK`, inscribe the :g:`A`-mixtilinear circle :g:`cF` tangent to sides :g:`AB`, :g:`AC` and internally to :g:`cK`. The line through :g:`K` parallel to :g:`AB` meets :g:`BC` at :g:`O` and :g:`cF` at :g:`I` and :g:`J` such that :math:`|KO|/|OJ| = \phi`.

Given & Constructive Steps
--------------------------

**Given**: Equilateral triangle :g:`ABC` of side :math:`18` with :g:`A` :math:`= (-9,0)`, :g:`B` :math:`= (9,0)`, :g:`C` :math:`= (0, 9\sqrt{3})`, and circumcenter :g:`K` :math:`= (0, 3\sqrt{3})`.

.. step::

   Draw circumcircle :g:`cK` of radius :math:`6\sqrt{3}` around :g:`ABC`.

.. step::

   Inscribe :g:`A`-mixtilinear circle :g:`cF` centered at :g:`F` :math:`= (3, 4\sqrt{3})` of radius :math:`4\sqrt{3}`, tangent to :g:`AB` at :g:`D` :math:`= (3,0)`.

.. step::

   Draw horizontal line :g:`KO` (:math:`y = 3\sqrt{3}`), cutting :g:`BC` at :g:`O` :math:`= (6, 3\sqrt{3})` and :g:`cF` at :g:`I` and :g:`J`.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate
----------------------------------

For side :math:`s = 18`, the :g:`A`-mixtilinear inradius is :math:`r_A = \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{9}s = 4\sqrt{3}` with :math:`|AD| = \frac{2}{3}s = 12`, placing :g:`F` at :math:`(3, 4\sqrt{3})`. Along :math:`y = 3\sqrt{3}`, :math:`(x - 3)^2 + (3\sqrt{3} - 4\sqrt{3})^2 = 48` yields :math:`(x - 3)^2 = 45`, so :math:`x_J = 3 + 3\sqrt{5}`. Since :math:`x_K = 0` and :math:`x_O = 6`, :math:`|KO| = 6` and :math:`|OJ| = 3(\sqrt{5} - 1) = 6/\phi`, proving :math:`|KO|/|OJ| = \phi`.
