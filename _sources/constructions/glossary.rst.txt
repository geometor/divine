Glossary of Geometric & Algebraic Terms
=======================================

Definitions of classical Euclidean, projective, and Rational Trigonometry concepts used throughout the catalog.

.. _term-golden-ratio:

**Golden Ratio (φ)** (*Sectio Aurea / Divina Proportione*)
   The positive root φ = (1 + √5)/2 ≈ 1.6180339887 of x² − x − 1 = 0, satisfying φ² = φ + 1 and 1/φ = φ − 1.
   *Appears in*: :doc:`GC-03.01 <03-squares-rectangles/01-tran-square-inscribed-semicircle/index>`, :doc:`GC-03.02 <03-squares-rectangles/02-tran-square-midpoints/index>`, :doc:`GC-03.03 <03-squares-rectangles/03-nguyen-2x2-square/index>`, :doc:`GC-03.04 <03-squares-rectangles/04-tran-mcgucken-3x3-circle/index>`, :doc:`GC-03.05 <03-squares-rectangles/05-tran-3x3-incenter/index>`, :doc:`GC-03.06 <03-squares-rectangles/06-suppa-square-inradii/index>`, :doc:`GC-03.07 <03-squares-rectangles/07-bui-cross-and-square/index>`, :doc:`GC-03.08 <03-squares-rectangles/08-arioni-rotating-square/index>`, :doc:`GC-04.01 <04-right-and-345/01-huntley-egyptian-345/index>`, :doc:`GC-04.02 <04-right-and-345/02-kepler-geometric-triangle/index>`, :doc:`GC-04.03 <04-right-and-345/03-niemeyer-three-segments/index>`, :doc:`GC-04.04 <04-right-and-345/04-bui-van-obel-isosceles/index>` (+19 more)

.. _term-golden-section:

**Golden Section** (*Collinear Division in φ*)
   A collinear point triple (A, G, B) whose consecutive segments stand in the golden ratio \|AG\|/\|GB\| = φ (so \|AB\|/\|AG\| = φ as well).
   *Appears in*: :doc:`GC-01.01 <01-minimal-compass/01-euclid-ii-11/index>`, :doc:`GC-01.02 <01-minimal-compass/02-pythagorean-transfer/index>`, :doc:`GC-01.03 <01-minimal-compass/03-lemoine-hofstetter-5step/index>`, :doc:`GC-01.04 <01-minimal-compass/04-hofstetter-5step-2004/index>`, :doc:`GC-01.06 <01-minimal-compass/06-hofstetter-rusty-compass/index>`, :doc:`GC-01.07 <01-minimal-compass/07-bui-5step/index>`, :doc:`GC-02.01 <02-equilateral-hexagon/01-odom-equilateral/index>`, :doc:`GC-02.02 <02-equilateral-hexagon/02-bataille-triangle-square/index>`, :doc:`GC-02.03 <02-equilateral-hexagon/03-bui-two-equilaterals/index>`, :doc:`GC-02.04 <02-equilateral-hexagon/04-tran-reflected-equilaterals/index>`, :doc:`GC-02.05 <02-equilateral-hexagon/05-tran-incircle-equilateral/index>`, :doc:`GC-02.06 <02-equilateral-hexagon/06-tran-hexagon-square/index>` (+32 more)

.. _term-extreme-and-mean-ratio:

**Extreme and Mean Ratio** (*ἄκρος καὶ μέσος λόγος (Euclid VI.Def.3)*)
   Euclid's classical definition: a straight line is cut in extreme and mean ratio when, as the whole line is to the greater segment, so is the greater to the less.
   *Appears in*: :doc:`GC-01.01 <01-minimal-compass/01-euclid-ii-11/index>`, :doc:`GC-05.01 <05-pentagons-polygons/01-ptolemy-pentagon-pentagram/index>`

.. _term-golden-chain:

**Golden Chain** (*Connected Collinear φ-Progression*)
   A sequence of four or more collinear points in which overlapping triples form consecutive golden sections (such as lengths 1/φ, 1, 1/φ or 1, φ, φ²).
   *Appears in*: :doc:`GC-01.02 <01-minimal-compass/02-pythagorean-transfer/index>`, :doc:`GC-01.03 <01-minimal-compass/03-lemoine-hofstetter-5step/index>`, :doc:`GC-01.05 <01-minimal-compass/05-hofstetter-compass-only/index>`, :doc:`GC-02.01 <02-equilateral-hexagon/01-odom-equilateral/index>`, :doc:`GC-02.07 <02-equilateral-hexagon/07-nguyen-butterfly-equilateral/index>`, :doc:`GC-03.01 <03-squares-rectangles/01-tran-square-inscribed-semicircle/index>`, :doc:`GC-03.07 <03-squares-rectangles/07-bui-cross-and-square/index>`, :doc:`GC-03.08 <03-squares-rectangles/08-arioni-rotating-square/index>`, :doc:`GC-05.01 <05-pentagons-polygons/01-ptolemy-pentagon-pentagram/index>`, :doc:`GC-05.03 <05-pentagons-polygons/03-prasolov-bui-irregular-pentagon/index>`, :doc:`GC-05.05 <05-pentagons-polygons/05-tran-star-six-pentagons/index>`, :doc:`GC-06.03 <06-circles-sangaku/03-bui-four-golden-circles/index>` (+5 more)

.. _term-quadrance:

**Quadrance** (*Q(A, B) = (x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²*)
   Norman Wildberger's Rational Trigonometry invariant measuring squared separation without square roots; two segments stand in ratio φ iff Q_long / Q_short = φ + 1 = φ².
   *Appears in*: :doc:`GC-01.01 <01-minimal-compass/01-euclid-ii-11/index>`, :doc:`GC-01.02 <01-minimal-compass/02-pythagorean-transfer/index>`, :doc:`GC-01.07 <01-minimal-compass/07-bui-5step/index>`, :doc:`GC-02.02 <02-equilateral-hexagon/02-bataille-triangle-square/index>`, :doc:`GC-02.03 <02-equilateral-hexagon/03-bui-two-equilaterals/index>`, :doc:`GC-02.04 <02-equilateral-hexagon/04-tran-reflected-equilaterals/index>`, :doc:`GC-02.06 <02-equilateral-hexagon/06-tran-hexagon-square/index>`, :doc:`GC-03.03 <03-squares-rectangles/03-nguyen-2x2-square/index>`, :doc:`GC-03.04 <03-squares-rectangles/04-tran-mcgucken-3x3-circle/index>`, :doc:`GC-03.08 <03-squares-rectangles/08-arioni-rotating-square/index>`, :doc:`GC-04.02 <04-right-and-345/02-kepler-geometric-triangle/index>`, :doc:`GC-04.03 <04-right-and-345/03-niemeyer-three-segments/index>` (+2 more)

.. _term-geometrographie:

**Géométrographie** (*Lemoine Construction Complexity*)
   Émile Lemoine's 1902 operational metric counting elementary straightedge (R₁, R₂) and compass (C₁, C₂, C₃) operations; drawing a line through two points or a circle centered at one point through another each contributes simplicity 3.
   *Appears in*: :doc:`GC-01.03 <01-minimal-compass/03-lemoine-hofstetter-5step/index>`, :doc:`GC-01.04 <01-minimal-compass/04-hofstetter-5step-2004/index>`, :doc:`GC-01.07 <01-minimal-compass/07-bui-5step/index>`

.. _term-vesica-piscis:

**Vesica Piscis** (*Twin-Circle Lens (Euclid I.1)*)
   The symmetric lens formed by two congruent circles each passing through the other's center, generating √3 altitudes and bisecting their common radius.
   *Appears in*: :doc:`GC-01.03 <01-minimal-compass/03-lemoine-hofstetter-5step/index>`, :doc:`GC-01.04 <01-minimal-compass/04-hofstetter-5step-2004/index>`, :doc:`GC-01.05 <01-minimal-compass/05-hofstetter-compass-only/index>`, :doc:`GC-01.06 <01-minimal-compass/06-hofstetter-rusty-compass/index>`, :doc:`GC-06.03 <06-circles-sangaku/03-bui-four-golden-circles/index>`

.. _term-mascheroni-construction:

**Mascheroni Construction** (*Compass-Only Construction*)
   A geometric construction using circles alone (0 straightedge lines), proved universal for all Euclidean constructible points by Georg Mohr (1672) and Lorenzo Mascheroni (1797).
   *Appears in*: :doc:`GC-01.05 <01-minimal-compass/05-hofstetter-compass-only/index>`

.. _term-rusty-compass:

**Rusty Compass** (*Fixed-Aperture Compass*)
   A compass locked to a single invariant radius throughout a construction, studied by Abu'l-Wafa, Leonardo da Vinci, Poncelet, and Steiner.
   *Appears in*: :doc:`GC-01.06 <01-minimal-compass/06-hofstetter-rusty-compass/index>`

.. _term-power-of-a-point:

**Power of a Point** (*Euclid III.35–37 / Radical Invariant*)
   For a point P and circle O(r), the invariant quantity \|PO\|² − r², equal to the signed product \|PA\|·\|PB\| along any secant PAB (and to \|PT\|² along a tangent PT).
   *Appears in*: :doc:`GC-02.05 <02-equilateral-hexagon/05-tran-incircle-equilateral/index>`, :doc:`GC-04.01 <04-right-and-345/01-huntley-egyptian-345/index>`, :doc:`GC-06.02 <06-circles-sangaku/02-mcgucken-kalogerakis-equal-circles/index>`, :doc:`GC-06.06 <06-circles-sangaku/06-arioni-yin-yang/index>`

.. _term-intersecting-chords-theorem:

**Intersecting Chords Theorem** (*Euclid Elements III.35*)
   If two chords AB and CD of a circle intersect internally at M, then \|AM\|·\|MB\| = \|CM\|·\|MD\|.
   *Appears in*: :doc:`GC-02.01 <02-equilateral-hexagon/01-odom-equilateral/index>`, :doc:`GC-03.01 <03-squares-rectangles/01-tran-square-inscribed-semicircle/index>`, :doc:`GC-06.04 <06-circles-sangaku/04-tran-mcgucken-three-tangent-circles/index>`

.. _term-butterfly-theorem:

**Butterfly Configuration** (*Crossed Cyclic Chords across a Midpoint Chord*)
   Cyclic quadrilateral diagonals crossing a reference chord symmetrically, inducing projective cross-ratio and golden-section divisions.
   *Appears in*: :doc:`GC-02.07 <02-equilateral-hexagon/07-nguyen-butterfly-equilateral/index>`

.. _term-kepler-triangle:

**Kepler Triangle** (*1 : √φ : φ Right Triangle*)
   The unique right triangle whose side lengths (and side quadrances 1 : φ : φ²) form a geometric progression.
   *Appears in*: :doc:`GC-04.02 <04-right-and-345/02-kepler-geometric-triangle/index>`, :doc:`GC-06.05 <06-circles-sangaku/05-kocik-golden-window/index>`

.. _term-mixtilinear-incircle:

**Mixtilinear Incircle** (*Two-Side & Circumcircle Tangent Circle*)
   A circle internally tangent to two sides of a triangle and internally tangent to its circumcircle.
   *Appears in*: :doc:`GC-07.05 <07-rhombi-trapezoids-algebra/05-tran-mixtilinear-equilateral/index>`

.. _term-golden-rhombus:

**Golden Rhombus** (*Diagonal Ratio |d₁|/|d₂| = φ*)
   A rhombus whose long and short diagonals stand in the golden ratio φ; thirty congruent golden rhombi bound Kepler's rhombic triacontahedron.
   *Appears in*: :doc:`GC-07.02 <07-rhombi-trapezoids-algebra/02-molokach-golden-rhombus/index>`

.. _term-golden-rectangle:

**Golden Rectangle** (*Side Ratio φ : 1*)
   A rectangle whose length-to-width ratio equals φ, characterized self-similarly by leaving a congruent-proportioned golden rectangle when a square gnomon is removed.
   *Appears in*: :doc:`GC-03.07 <03-squares-rectangles/07-bui-cross-and-square/index>`, :doc:`GC-06.05 <06-circles-sangaku/05-kocik-golden-window/index>`, :doc:`GC-07.06 <07-rhombi-trapezoids-algebra/06-curry-hooper-dissection/index>`
