GC-04 · Right Triangles, Egyptian 3–4–5 & Kepler Proportions
============================================================

*Huntley's 3–4–5 bisector circle, Kepler's geometric-progression right triangle, and Niemeyer's linkage*

In a 1597 letter to Michael Mästlin, :person:`Johannes Kepler <johannes-kepler>` called the Pythagorean theorem and the division in extreme and mean ratio the 'two great treasures of geometry.' This family unites both treasures directly: :person:`H. E. Huntley <h-e-huntley>`'s angle-bisector circle in the :math:`3\text{-}4\text{-}5` Egyptian triangle, Kepler's unique :math:`1 : \sqrt{\phi} : \phi` right triangle over :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{\phi})`, :person:`Jo Niemeyer <jo-niemeyer>`'s three equal leaning rods, and Van Obel cevian concurrence.
