GC-05 · Pentagons, Pentagrams, Affine Invariance & Stars
========================================================

*Ptolemy's Almagest pentagon, Prasolov's irregular equi-area pentagon, six-pentagon star, and Wu Xing*

The regular pentagon and pentagram have embodied the divine proportion since Pythagorean antiquity and :person:`Claudius Ptolemy <claudius-ptolemy>`'s *Almagest*. Beyond regular fivefold symmetry, :person:`V. V. Prasolov <v-v-prasolov>` and :person:`Bùi Quang Tuân <bui-quang-tuan>` showed that *any* convex pentagon whose five peripheral triangles have equal area retains every golden diagonal ratio purely by affine invariance, while :person:`Dao Thanh Oai <dao-thanh-oai>` and :person:`Tran Quang Hung <tran-quang-hung>` revealed new fivefold star intersections.
