GC-06 · Circles, Apollonian Windows, Yin–Yang & Sangaku
=======================================================

*Infinite rational secants, tangent equal circles, Kocik's Golden Window, Yin–Yang, and Sangaku packings*

Tangent circle packings translate tangency constraints into quadratic Descartes–Pythagoras equations whose positive roots repeatedly evaluate to :math:`\phi`. This family catalogs :person:`Bùi Quang Tuân <bui-quang-tuan>`'s infinite Diophantine secant pencil and four-circle vesica chain, tangent circle theorems by :person:`Elliot McGucken <elliot-mcgucken>` and :person:`Thanos Kalogerakis <thanos-kalogerakis>`, :person:`Jerzy Kocik <jerzy-kocik>`'s six-circle Apollonian *Golden Window*, :person:`John Arioni <john-arioni>`'s Yin–Yang secant, and three equal circles inscribed in a semicircle.
