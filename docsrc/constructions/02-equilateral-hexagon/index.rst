GC-02 · Equilateral Triangles & Regular Hexagons
================================================

*George Odom's midline chord, reflected equilaterals, Bataille's hybrid, and butterfly chords*

At first glance, threefold (:math:`60^\circ`) symmetry lives in :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3})`, algebraically independent of :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\phi)`. Yet whenever an equilateral midline, circumcircle secant, or unit square interacts with a :math:`60^\circ` lattice—beginning with artist :person:`George Odom <george-odom>`'s celebrated 1983 discovery communicated to :person:`H. S. M. Coxeter <h-s-m-coxeter>`—quadratic chord products generate :math:`\sqrt{5}` and :math:`\phi` inside :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \phi)`.
