Tran Quang Hung
===============

**Tran Quang Hung** (fl. 2012 – present) — *Vietnamese Geometer; Hanoi University of Science High School*
   Contributed an extraordinary suite of synthetic Golden Ratio discoveries across equilateral triangles, squares, 3×3 grids, hexagons, pentagon stars, Wu Xing rotations, rhombi, trapezoids, and mixtilinear circles.
