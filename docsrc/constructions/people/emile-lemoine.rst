Emile Lemoine
=============

**Émile Michel Hyacinthe Lemoine** (1840 – 1912) — *French Geometer; Founder of Géométrographie*
   Founded Géométrographie (1902), a quantitative operational calculus measuring the simplicity and exactitude of ruler-and-compass constructions, and recorded early minimal divisions in extreme and mean ratio.
   *Cataloged constructions*: :doc:`GC-01.03 <../01-minimal-compass/03-lemoine-hofstetter-5step/index>`
