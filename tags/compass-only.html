GC-01.05 · Mascheroni Five-Circle Compass-Only Construction

Using a compass alone and zero straightedge lines, five circles constructed from given points A and B generate four collinear points Y, C, D, X such that \(|CX|/|CD| = |CD|/|DX| = \phi\).
