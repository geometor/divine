GC-02.01 · George Odom’s Equilateral Midline & Circumcircle Chord (1983)

Let ABC be an equilateral triangle inscribed in circle cO, and let L and M be the midpoints of sides AB and AC. Extending midline LM to meet circumcircle cO at X and Y divides the chord in the golden ratio: \(|LM|/|MY| = |LY|/|LM| = \phi\).
