GC-06.06 · John Arioni’s Golden Secant in the Yin-Yang Circle

Inscribe twin tangent circles cA and cC of radius \(\tfrac{1}{2}\) along horizontal diameter PLPR of unit circle cO, and let B be the apex of perpendicular radius OB. Secant BA cuts cA at H and I such that \(|HI|/|BH| = |BI|/|HI| = \phi\).

GC-03.08 · John Arioni’s 30°-Rotated Unit Square & Diagonal Circle

Rotate unit square DGHJ clockwise by \(30^\circ\) around vertex D to obtain congruent square DCBA. Circle cAC centered at A through opposite vertex C intersects line DG at E and F such that \(|DG|/|GE| = |DE|/|DG| = \phi\).
