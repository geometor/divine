GC-01.03 · Lemoine–Reusch–Hofstetter 5-Step Division

Given points A and B on line AB, let unit vesica circles cAB and cBA meet at C and D. Circle cCA cuts cAB at E and chord CD at F; then circle cEF cuts AB internally at G and externally at G1 so that \(|AG|/|GB| = |G_1A|/|AB| = \phi\).
