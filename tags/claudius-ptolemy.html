GC-05.01 · Claudius Ptolemy’s Regular Pentagon & Pentagram (Almagest I.10)

In unit circle cO with perpendicular radii OB and OC, let M bisect OB. Circle cMC cuts AB at E with \(|OB|/|EO| = \phi\) and chord \(|CE|\) equal to the side of inscribed regular pentagon CP1P2P3P4, whose pentagram diagonals P1P4, CP2, CP3 cut one another in golden chains \(|P1K2|/|K2P4| = \phi\).
