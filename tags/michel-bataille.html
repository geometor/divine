GC-02.02 · Michel Bataille’s Equilateral Triangle & External Square

Erect equilateral triangle ABC and external square BCDE on opposite sides of shared base BC. Circle cCE centered at C through opposite square vertex E cuts ray AB beyond B at F so that \(|AF|/|AB| = |AB|/|BF| = \phi\).
