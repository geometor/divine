Types / person

Content of type 'person'

Alexander Bogomolny

Founded Interactive Mathematics Miscellany and Puzzles (Cut-the-Knot, 1996–2018), curating, proving, and illustrating dozens of classical and contemporary Golden Ratio constructions contributed by geometers worldwide. Cataloged constructions: GC-06.07, GC-07.05, GC-07.06

Alexandre Borovik

Observed the golden rectangle embedded inside Bùi Quang Tuân’s concentric Greek cross and square configuration on Cut-the-Knot. Cataloged constructions: GC-03.07

Bui Quang Tuan

Discovered numerous synthetic Golden Ratio theorems curated on Cut-the-Knot, including a 5-step collinear circle construction, the Greek Cross & Square, Four Golden Circles, Van Obel isosceles division, and infinite circle-secant families. Cataloged constructions: GC-01.07, GC-02.03, GC-03.07, GC-04.04, GC-05.03, GC-06.01, GC-06.03

Claudius Ptolemy

Introduced the streamlined circle-and-midpoint construction of the regular decagon and pentagon in Almagest Book I, Chapter 10 to compute his trigonometric table of chords. Cataloged constructions: GC-05.01

Dao Thanh Oai

Known for discoveries in Euclidean and projective triangle/polygon geometry, including the regular-pentagon edge-perpendicular division in φ. Cataloged constructions: GC-05.02

Elliot Mcgucken

Contributed golden-section constructions in two and three tangent equal circles and 3×3 square grids on Cut-the-Knot. Cataloged constructions: GC-03.04, GC-06.02, GC-06.04

Emile Lemoine

Founded Géométrographie (1902), a quantitative operational calculus measuring the simplicity and exactitude of ruler-and-compass constructions, and recorded early minimal divisions in extreme and mean ratio. Cataloged constructions: GC-01.03

Ercole Suppa

Researcher in triangle centers and sangaku-style circle configurations; discovered the golden ratio of inradii in a midpoint-partitioned square (2015). Cataloged constructions: GC-03.06

Euclid

Formulated division in extreme and mean ratio in Elements Book II Proposition 11 and Book VI Proposition 30, applying it to construct the regular pentagon (IV.11) and the icosahedron and dodecahedron (Book XIII). Cataloged constructions: GC-01.01, GC-01.02, GC-05.01

George Odom

Discovered the celebrated two-line equilateral-triangle midline construction of φ (1982–1983) through visual exploration of polyhedra and three-dimensional models. Cataloged constructions: GC-02.01

Gregoire Nicollier

Contributed analytic and synthetic proofs of polygonal and equilateral Golden Ratio configurations on Cut-the-Knot.

H E Huntley

Author of The Divine Proportion: A Study in Mathematical Beauty (Dover, 1970), highlighting unexpected appearances of φ such as the angle-bisector circle in the 3-4-5 right triangle. Cataloged constructions: GC-04.01

H S M Coxeter

Communicated George Odom’s equilateral midline golden-section problem in the American Mathematical Monthly (Problem E3007, 1983) and championed classical synthetic and projective geometry. Cataloged constructions: GC-02.01

Jerzy Kocik

Discovered and analyzed the Apollonian ‘Golden Window’ circle packing (Mathematics Magazine, 2010), showing how simultaneous tangency forces powers of φ. Cataloged constructions: GC-06.05

Jingcheng Tong

Characterized area-bisecting chords in trapezoids whose parallel bases stand in ratio 1 : 3, dividing both legs in the golden ratio. Cataloged constructions: GC-07.03

Jo Niemeyer

Constructivist artist whose sculptures and geometric studies feature the golden division generated by three equal leaning segments. Cataloged constructions: GC-04.03

Johannes Kepler

Described the Pythagorean theorem and division in extreme and mean ratio as the ‘two great treasures of geometry’ (1597 letter to Michael Mästlin) and studied the 1 : √φ : φ right triangle and rhombic triacontahedron. Cataloged constructions: GC-04.02

John Arioni

Contributed the Yin-Yang twin-circle secant construction and the 30°-rotated unit square diagonal-circle construction of φ on Cut-the-Knot. Cataloged constructions: GC-03.08, GC-06.06

John Molokach

Devised the construction of a golden rhombus and consecutive powers of φ from two unit-shifted 1×3 rectangles and an inscribed unit circle. Cataloged constructions: GC-07.02

Kurt Hofstetter

Published a remarkable series of minimal 5-step Euclidean, 5-circle Mascheroni compass-only, and fixed-opening rusty-compass Golden Ratio constructions in Forum Geometricorum (2002–2005). Cataloged constructions: GC-01.03, GC-01.04, GC-01.05, GC-01.06

Leo Giugiuc

Co-solved and generalized several trigonometric, mixtilinear, and butterfly Golden Ratio problems curated by Alexander Bogomolny. Cataloged constructions: GC-07.05

Luca Pacioli

Chronicled thirteen ‘effects’ of extreme and mean ratio in De Divina Proportione (1509, illustrated by Leonardo da Vinci), popularizing the Renaissance title Divina Proportione.

Michel Bataille

Prolific contributor to Crux Mathematicorum and Forum Geometricorum; devised the equilateral-triangle-and-square radical-axis construction of φ (2011). Cataloged constructions: GC-02.02

Nguyen Thanh Dung

Contributed butterfly-chord equilateral constructions and 2×2 square lattice divisions in extreme and mean ratio on Cut-the-Knot. Cataloged constructions: GC-02.07, GC-03.03

Paul Curry

Invented the Curry Paradox (1953) of area-discrepant triangle and rectangle rearrangements driven by Fibonacci slopes approaching 1/φ. Cataloged constructions: GC-07.06

Quang Duong

Contributed synthetic proofs for Tran Quang Hung’s reflected equilateral and incircle Golden Ratio theorems.

Ron Knott

Creator of the encyclopedic Fibonacci Numbers and the Golden Section resource (cataloged in sibling repository geometor/knott).

Thanos Kalogerakis

Observed the diameter-to-secant golden ratio across two externally tangent equal circles. Cataloged constructions: GC-06.02

Tran Quang Hung

Contributed an extraordinary suite of synthetic Golden Ratio discoveries across equilateral triangles, squares, 3×3 grids, hexagons, pentagon stars, Wu Xing rotations, rhombi, trapezoids, and mixtilinear circles. Cataloged constructions: GC-02.04, GC-02.05, GC-02.06, GC-03.01, GC-03.02, GC-03.04, GC-03.05, GC-04.01, GC-05.02, GC-05.04, GC-05.05, GC-05.06, GC-06.04, GC-07.01, GC-07.04, GC-07.05

V V Prasolov

Author of Problems in Plane and Solid Geometry, documenting affine equi-area characterizations of golden diagonal ratios in general convex pentagons. Cataloged constructions: GC-05.03

William Hooper

Published the earliest known geometric missing-area rectangle dissection fallacy in Rational Recreations (1774/1794), whose vanishing-gap limit is governed by φ. Cataloged constructions: GC-07.06
