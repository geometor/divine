GC-03.04 · Tran Quang Hung & Elliot McGucken’s 3×3 Grid Circle¶

Discoverer(s): Tran Quang Hung, Elliot McGucken | Date: 2016 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \phi)\) | Loci: 2 (Lemoine \(S = 6\))

In a \(3 \times 3\) square grid S1AS3S4 centered at O, let C be the upper-right vertex of the central grid cell, A the outer upper-right corner, and T the adjacent trisection vertex on the top edge. Circle cOT cuts diagonal segment CA at B such that \(|CB|/|BA| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: A \(3 \times 3\) square grid on \([-3, 3]^2\) centered at O \(= (0,0)\) with outer vertex A \(= (3,3)\).

step Identify central cell corner C \(= (1,1)\) and top boundary grid node T \(= (1,3)\).
step Draw diagonal line OA through C and A.
step Draw circle cOT centered at O through T, cutting segment CA at B.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

Circle cOT has quadrance radius \(Q(O, T) = 1^2 + 3^2 = 10\). Along diagonal \(y = x\), \(2x^2 = 10\) gives B \(= (\sqrt{5}, \sqrt{5})\). Since collinear points \(C(1,1), B(\sqrt{5},\sqrt{5}), A(3,3)\) project linearly onto the \(x\)-axis, \(|CB|/|BA| = (\sqrt{5} - 1)/(3 - \sqrt{5}) = \phi\).
