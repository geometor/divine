GC-03.05 · Tran Quang Hung’s 3×3 Grid Incenter Division¶

Discoverer(s): Tran Quang Hung | Date: 2016 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \phi)\) | Loci: 2 (Lemoine \(S = 6\))

In a \(3 \times 3\) grid AQ1Q2Q3 on \([0,3]^2\), let S \(= (1,1)\) and T \(= (2,2)\) be opposite corners of the central unit cell, and let U \(= (3,1)\) and V \(= (1,3)\). The incenter P of isosceles triangle AUV divides diagonal ST of the central cell in the golden ratio: \(|PT|/|SP| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Outer \(3 \times 3\) square AQ1Q2Q3 with A \(= (0,0)\) and Q1 \(= (3,0)\).

step Mark central cell diagonal vertices S \(= (1,1)\) and T \(= (2,2)\) along symmetry axis AT.
step Form lattice isosceles triangle AUV with U \(= (3,1)\) and V \(= (1,3)\) (whose base UV has midpoint T).
step Construct incenter P and incircle cPT tangent to UV at T.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

Triangle AUV has side lengths \(|AU| = |AV| = \sqrt{10}\) and \(|UV| = 2\sqrt{2}\). By the angle-bisector theorem on altitude AT (\(|AT| = 2\sqrt{2}\)), \(|PT|/|AP| = (|UV|/2)/|AU| = \sqrt{2}/\sqrt{10} = 1/\sqrt{5}\), so \(x_P = 2 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{5}}{\sqrt{5} + 1} = \frac{5 - \sqrt{5}}{2}\). Hence \(2 - x_P = \frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2} = 1/\phi\) and \(x_P - 1 = \frac{3-\sqrt{5}}{2} = 1/\phi^2\), proving \(|PT|/|SP| = \phi\).
