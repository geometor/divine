GC-03.06 · Ercole Suppa’s Square Midpoint Inradii Ratio (2015)¶

Discoverer(s): Ercole Suppa | Date: 2015 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\) | Loci: 4 (Lemoine \(S = 12\))

In square ABCD, let M be the midpoint of side AD. The inradii \(R\) and \(r\) of triangles \(\triangle BCM\) (incircle cEH) and \(\triangle CDM\) (incircle cFP) satisfy \(R / r = \phi\), and segment EF joining their incenters is divided by CM at K with \(|EK|/|KF| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Square ABCD of side \(2\) with D \(= (0,0)\), C \(= (2,0)\), B \(= (2,2)\), A \(= (0,2)\) and midpoint M \(= (0,1)\).

step Join CM and BM, partitioning the lower and right regions into right \(\triangle CDM\) and isosceles \(\triangle BCM\).
step Inscribe circle cEH in \(\triangle BCM\) (incenter E, tangent to BC at H) and circle cFP in \(\triangle CDM\) (incenter F, tangent to CD at P).
step Join incenters EF to intersect shared side CM at K.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

For \(\triangle BCM\), area \(= 2\) and semiperimeter \(s_1 = 1 + \sqrt{5}\) give inradius \(R = \frac{2}{\sqrt{5}+1} = \frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2} = 1/\phi\). For right \(\triangle CDM\) with legs \(1, 2\) and hypotenuse \(\sqrt{5}\), inradius \(r = \frac{1 + 2 - \sqrt{5}}{2} = \frac{3 - \sqrt{5}}{2} = 1/\phi^2\). Since E and F lie at perpendicular distances \(R\) and \(r\) on opposite sides of shared line CM, similar right triangles yield \(|EK|/|KF| = R/r = \phi\).

Historical Commentary & Attribution¶

Suppa’s theorem exhibits consecutive negative powers of \(\phi\) (\(\phi^{-1}\) and \(\phi^{-2}\), whose sum \(\phi^{-1} + \phi^{-2} = 1\) equals half the side of the square) as exact Euclidean inradii.
