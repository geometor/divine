GC-03.08 · John Arioni’s 30°-Rotated Unit Square & Diagonal Circle¶

Discoverer(s): John Arioni | Date: 2016 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \phi)\) | Loci: 2 (Lemoine \(S = 6\))

Rotate unit square DGHJ clockwise by \(30^\circ\) around vertex D to obtain congruent square DCBA. Circle cAC centered at A through opposite vertex C intersects line DG at E and F such that \(|DG|/|GE| = |DE|/|DG| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Unit square DGHJ on base D \(= (0,0)\), G \(= (1,0)\).

step Rotate DGHJ clockwise by \(30^\circ\) about D so A \(= (\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})\) and C \(= (\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, -\tfrac{1}{2})\).
step Draw diagonal AC (of length \(\sqrt{2}\)) of the rotated unit square DCBA.
step Draw circle cAC centered at A through C, cutting base line DG at E and F.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

Center A has altitude \(y_A = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\) above line DG (\(y = 0\)) and abscissa \(x_A = \frac{1}{2}\), while radius \(|AC| = \sqrt{2}\). Setting \(y = 0\) in \((x - \tfrac{1}{2})^2 + (\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})^2 = 2\) gives \((x - \tfrac{1}{2})^2 = \frac{5}{4}\), hence \(x_E = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2} = \phi\) and \(x_F = -1/\phi\), forming the 4-point golden chain \((F, D, G, E)\).
