GC-03.01 · Tran Quang Hung’s Square Inscribed in a Right Isosceles Semicircle¶

Discoverer(s): Tran Quang Hung | Date: 2014 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\) | Loci: 3 (Lemoine \(S = 9\))

Let ABC be a right isosceles triangle inscribed in a semicircle of cO on diameter AB, and let square DEGF sit on AB with vertices D and E on legs AC and BC. Extending top side DE to meet circle cO at Q and P divides DP in the golden ratio: \(|DE|/|EP| = |DP|/|DE| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Circle cO centered at O \(= (0,0)\) of radius \(3\) with diameter A \(= (-3,0)\), B \(= (3,0)\) and apex C \(= (0,3)\).

step Inscribe right isosceles triangle ABC in the upper semicircle on diameter AB.
step Inscribe square DEGF of side \(2\) with F \(= (-1,0)\), G \(= (1,0)\) on AB and D \(= (-1,2)\), E \(= (1,2)\) on AC, BC.
step Extend line DE horizontally to meet circle cO at Q \(= (-\sqrt{5}, 2)\) and P \(= (\sqrt{5}, 2)\).

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

At \(x = \pm 1\), legs AC (\(y = x + 3\)) and BC (\(y = -x + 3\)) have height \(y = 2\), matching the horizontal side \(|DE| = 2\) of inscribed square DEGF. On circle \(x^2 + y^2 = 9\), setting \(y = 2\) yields \(x = \pm\sqrt{5}\), so \(|EP| = \sqrt{5} - 1 = 2/\phi\) and \(|DE|/|EP| = 2 / (2/\phi) = \phi\).
