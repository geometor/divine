GC-03.07 · Bùi Quang Tuân & Alexandre Borovik’s Greek Cross & Square¶

Discoverer(s): Bùi Quang Tuân, Alexandre Borovik | Date: 2015 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\) | Loci: 4 (Lemoine \(S = 12\))

Inscribe a symmetrical five-square Greek cross CROSS and a concentric axis-aligned square S1S2S3S4 in the same circle cO. Along any inner arm line AC of the cross, the square edge S1S2 cuts AC at B such that \(|BC|/|AB| = \phi\), while \(|BD|/|CD| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Circle cO centered at O \(= (0,0)\) through cross corner A \(= (1,3)\) (radius \(\sqrt{10}\)).

step Inscribe the five-square Greek cross CROSS of square side \(2\), with right upper arm edge passing through A \(= (1,3)\), C \(= (1,1)\), and D \(= (1,-1)\).
step Inscribe concentric square S1S2S3S4 of side \(2\sqrt{5}\) with vertices \((\pm\sqrt{5}, \pm\sqrt{5})\) on cO.
step Mark B \(= (1, \sqrt{5})\) and E \(= (1, -\sqrt{5})\) where horizontal square edges S1S2 and S4S3 meet line AC.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

Each cross arm has half-width \(1\) and outer reach \(3\), so \(R^2 = 1^2 + 3^2 = 10\). An axis-aligned square in the same circle satisfies \(x^2 + y^2 = 10\) with \(|x| = |y| = \sqrt{5}\). On vertical line \(x = 1\), points \(A(1,3), B(1,\sqrt{5}), C(1,1), D(1,-1)\) have \(|BC| = \sqrt{5} - 1 = 2/\phi\), \(|AB| = 3 - \sqrt{5} = 2/\phi^2\), and \(|BD| = \sqrt{5} + 1 = 2\phi\), giving both \(|BC|/|AB| = \phi\) and \(|BD|/|CD| = \phi\).
