GC-03.02 · Tran Quang Hung’s Square Midpoint Circle Division¶

Discoverer(s): Tran Quang Hung | Date: 2015 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\) | Loci: 4 (Lemoine \(S = 12\))

Let ABCD be a square with center F and side midpoints M, N, P, Q. The circle cKA on diameter AP intersects midline QN at X between F and N such that \(|FX|/|XN| = \phi\), while chord AX cuts MP at Z with \(|MZ|/|ZF| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Square ABCD of side \(4\) centered at F \(= (0,0)\) with vertices \((\pm 2, \pm 2)\).

step Mark side midpoints M \(= (0,2)\), N \(= (2,0)\), P \(= (0,-2)\), Q \(= (-2,0)\) and center F \(= (0,0)\).
step Bisect AP at K \(= (-1,0)\) and draw circle cKA of radius \(\sqrt{5}\) on diameter AP.
step Mark X where cKA crosses QN between F and N, and Z where AX crosses MP.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

Circle cKA has equation \((x + 1)^2 + y^2 = 5\). Along QN (\(y = 0\)), the positive root is \(x_X = \sqrt{5} - 1 = 2/\phi\). Since \(x_N = 2\), \(|XN| = 3 - \sqrt{5} = 2/\phi^2\), whence \(|FX|/|XN| = (2/\phi)/(2/\phi^2) = \phi\).

\(Q(F, X) = 6 - 2 \sqrt{5}\), \(Q(X, N) = 14 - 6 \sqrt{5}\), \(Q_{\text{long}}/Q_{\text{short}} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2} + \frac{3}{2} = \phi^2\)
