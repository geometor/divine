GC-03.03 · Nguyen Thanh Dung’s 2×2 Grid Compass Arc¶

Discoverer(s): Nguyen Thanh Dung | Date: 2016 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\) | Loci: 2 (Lemoine \(S = 6\))

On a \(2 \times 2\) square grid P1P2P3P4 with horizontal midline AB, center O, and top midpoint D, extend AB by one grid unit to C. Circle cCD centered at C through D cuts AB at M in the golden ratio: \(|MB|/|AM| = |AB|/|MB| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: A \(2 \times 2\) grid with midline endpoints A \(= (-1,0)\) and B \(= (1,0)\).

step Form the \(2 \times 2\) grid P1P2P3P4 centered at O \(= (0,0)\) with top midpoint D \(= (0,1)\).
step Mark C \(= (2,0)\) on ray AB at unit distance beyond B.
step Draw circle cCD centered at C through D, intersecting AB internally at M.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

In right triangle \(\triangle COD\), legs \(|OC| = 2\) and \(|OD| = 1\) give radius \(|CD| = \sqrt{5}\). Hence \(x_M = 2 - \sqrt{5}\), giving \(|MB| = 1 - (2 - \sqrt{5}) = \sqrt{5} - 1 = 2/\phi\) and \(|AM| = (2 - \sqrt{5}) - (-1) = 3 - \sqrt{5} = 2/\phi^2\), so \(|MB|/|AM| = \phi\).
