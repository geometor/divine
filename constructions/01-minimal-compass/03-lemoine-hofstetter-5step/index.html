GC-01.03 · Lemoine–Reusch–Hofstetter 5-Step Division¶

Discoverer(s): Émile Michel Hyacinthe Lemoine, Kurt Hofstetter | Date: 1902 / 2003 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \phi)\) | Loci: 6 (Lemoine \(S = 18\))

Given points A and B on line AB, let unit vesica circles cAB and cBA meet at C and D. Circle cCA cuts cAB at E and chord CD at F; then circle cEF cuts AB internally at G and externally at G1 so that \(|AG|/|GB| = |G_1A|/|AB| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Unit base points A \(= (0, 0)\) and B \(= (1, 0)\) on carrier line AB.

step Draw circles cAB and cBA of unit radius, intersecting at C \(= (\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})\) and D \(= (\tfrac{1}{2}, -\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})\).
step Join perpendicular bisector line CD.
step Draw circle cCA centered at C through A, intersecting cAB at E \(= (-\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})\) and segment CD at F \(= (\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} - 1)\).
step Draw circle cEF centered at E through F, cutting AB internally at G and externally at G1.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

Because E and C share ordinate \(\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\) at horizontal separation \(1\), while F lies \(1\) unit directly below C, triangle \(\triangle ECF\) is right isosceles with \(Q(E, F) = 1^2 + 1^2 = 2\). Circle cEF therefore satisfies \((x + \tfrac{1}{2})^2 + y^2 = 2\); setting \(y = 0\) yields \((x + \tfrac{1}{2})^2 = 2 - \tfrac{3}{4} = \tfrac{5}{4}\), giving G \(= (1/\phi, 0)\) and G1 \(= (-\phi, 0)\). Thus \(|AG|/|GB| = \phi\) and \((G_1, A, G, B)\) forms a connected golden chain along AB.

Historical Commentary & Attribution¶

Rediscovered independently by Kurt Hofstetter (Hofstetter 2003), this five-locus division of a pre-drawn line segment had appeared a century earlier in Émile Lemoine’s Géométrographie (Lemoine 1902) and J. Reusch’s Planymetrische Konstruktionen in geometrographischer Ausführung (1904).
