GC-01.07 · Bùi Quang Tuân’s Five-Step Three-Circle Secant¶

Discoverer(s): Bùi Quang Tuân | Date: 2014 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5}, \sqrt{7})\) | Loci: 5 (Lemoine \(S = 15\))

On line L through O, draw circle cO meeting L at A and B, circle cBA meeting L at C, and circle cCO meeting cO above L at M and cBA below L at N. Then chord MN cuts cO at P with \(|MN|/|MP| = |MP|/|PN| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Center O \(= (0, 0)\) and unit point B \(= (-1, 0)\) defining carrier line L.

step Draw line L through O and B, and unit circle cO meeting L again at A \(= (1, 0)\).
step Draw circle cBA of radius \(2\) centered at B through A, meeting L beyond B at C \(= (-3, 0)\).
step Draw circle cCO of radius \(3\) centered at C through O, cutting cO above L at M \(= (-\tfrac{1}{6}, \tfrac{\sqrt{35}}{6})\) and cBA below L at N \(= (-\tfrac{3}{4}, -\tfrac{3\sqrt{7}}{4})\).
step Join MN, intersecting unit circle cO a second time at P.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

Direct quadrance computation between M and N yields \(Q(M, N) = (\tfrac{7}{12})^2 + (\tfrac{\sqrt{35}}{6} + \tfrac{3\sqrt{7}}{4})^2 = \tfrac{21}{4} + \tfrac{7\sqrt{5}}{4} = \tfrac{7}{2}\phi^2\). Because O, M, and N all lie on radius-\(3\) circle cCO while M and P lie on unit circle cO, inscribed-angle similarity with diameter endpoint \((-6, 0)\) of cCO gives \(Q(M, P) = \tfrac{7}{2}\) and \(Q(P, N) = \tfrac{7}{2}\phi^{-2}\). Hence \(Q(M, P) / Q(P, N) = \phi^2\) and \(|MP|/|PN| = \phi\).

Historical Commentary & Attribution¶

Devised by Bùi Quang Tuân (Cut-the-Knot), this five-step construction starts from an arbitrary radius on line L rather than a pre-specified unit segment, generating \(\phi\) inside the biquadratic field \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5}, \sqrt{7})\).
