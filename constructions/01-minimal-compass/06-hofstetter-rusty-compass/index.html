GC-01.06 · Hofstetter’s Fixed-Aperture Rusty-Compass Division¶

Discoverer(s): Kurt Hofstetter | Date: 2005 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \phi)\) | Loci: 6 (Lemoine \(S = 18\))

With a rusty compass locked permanently to aperture \(r = |AB|\), circles cAB and cBA determine midpoint M via CD; a third unit circle cM centered at M meets cBA above AB at F, and line DF cuts AB at G in the golden ratio \(|AG|/|GB| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Unit segment endpoints A \(= (0, 0)\) and B \(= (1, 0)\) and a fixed compass radius \(r = 1\).

step Draw line AB and unit circles cAB, cBA meeting at C and D.
step Join CD to bisect AB at M \(= (\tfrac{1}{2}, 0)\).
step With the unchanged compass opening \(r = 1\), draw circle cM centered at M, meeting cBA farthest from D at F \(= (\tfrac{3}{4}, \tfrac{\sqrt{15}}{4})\).
step Join DF to intersect AB at G \(= (1/\phi, 0)\).

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

Unit circles cM \(((x - \tfrac{1}{2})^2 + y^2 = 1)\) and cBA \(((x - 1)^2 + y^2 = 1)\) intersect along their radical axis \(x = \tfrac{3}{4}\), giving F \(= (\tfrac{3}{4}, \tfrac{\sqrt{15}}{4})\)—the exact same upper intersection point achieved by radius-\(2\) circle \(E(B)\) in Hofstetter’s 2004 construction! Hence line DF meets AB at G \(= (1/\phi, 0)\), so \(|AG|/|GB| = \phi\).

Historical Commentary & Attribution¶

Poncelet–Steiner and Abū al-Wafāʾ tradition prizes constructions executed with a single fixed compass opening (compas rouillé). Kurt Hofstetter (Hofstetter 2005, Cut-the-Knot) requires only three unit circles and three lines.
