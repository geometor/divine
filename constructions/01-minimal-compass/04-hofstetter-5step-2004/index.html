GC-01.04 · Hofstetter’s 2004 Five-Step Secant Division¶

Discoverer(s): Kurt Hofstetter | Date: 2004 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \phi)\) | Loci: 5 (Lemoine \(S = 15\))

Given points A and B, draw line AB and unit circles cAB, cBA meeting at C and D, with cAB meeting ray BA beyond A at E. Circle cEB cuts cBA above AB at F, and secant DF divides AB at G in the golden ratio \(|AG|/|GB| = \phi\) in exactly five Euclidean loci.

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Points A \(= (0, 0)\) and B \(= (1, 0)\) in the Euclidean plane.

step Draw carrier line AB (Locus 1).
step Draw circle cAB centered at A through B (Locus 2), meeting line AB beyond A at E \(= (-1, 0)\).
step Draw circle cBA centered at B through A (Locus 3), intersecting cAB at C and D \(= (\tfrac{1}{2}, -\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})\).
step Draw circle cEB centered at E through B of radius \(2\) (Locus 4), meeting cBA above AB at F \(= (\tfrac{3}{4}, \tfrac{\sqrt{15}}{4})\).
step Draw line DF (Locus 5), cutting AB at G \(= (1/\phi, 0)\).

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

Subtracting \((x - 1)^2 + y^2 = 1\) from \((x + 1)^2 + y^2 = 4\) gives \(4x = 3\), so F has exact coordinates \((\tfrac{3}{4}, \tfrac{\sqrt{15}}{4})\). Along secant DF joining \((\tfrac{1}{2}, -\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})\) to \((\tfrac{3}{4}, \tfrac{\sqrt{15}}{4})\), the \(x\)-intercept G is \(x_G = \tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{1}{4} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}/2}{\sqrt{3}/2 + \sqrt{15}/4} = \tfrac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2(\sqrt{5} + 2)} = \frac{\sqrt{5} - 1}{2} = \frac{1}{\phi}\). Therefore \(|AG| = 1/\phi\), \(|GB| = 1/\phi^2\), and \(|AG|/|GB| = \phi\).

Historical Commentary & Attribution¶

Published in Forum Geometricorum (Hofstetter 2004), this construction requires only two lines and three circles even when the line through A and B is not drawn in advance. Notice that its abscissae \(x_D = \tfrac{1}{2}\) and \(x_F = \tfrac{3}{4}\) (or under reflection \(\tfrac{1}{4}, \tfrac{1}{2}\)) are a special case of Bùi Quang Tuân’s general bilinear circle theorem (Cut-the-Knot).
