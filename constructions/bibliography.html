Bibliography¶

Primary Treatises, Journal Articles & Cut-the-Knot Archival Sources

Primary mathematical treatises, peer-reviewed journal articles, and curated archival pages from Alexander Bogomolny’s Interactive Mathematics Miscellany and Puzzles (Cut-the-Knot, © 1996–2018).

Elements (Heath Edition) (c. 300 BCE)

Euclid — Book II Prop. 11; Book IV Prop. 10–11; Book VI Def. 3 & Prop. 30; Book XIII Prop. 1–10. Sir Thomas L. Heath, The Thirteen Books of Euclid’s Elements, Cambridge University Press, 1908 (GEOMETOR Digital Scriptorium edition). URL: https://geometor.github.io/euclid/ Cataloged in: GC-01.01, GC-05.01

Syntaxis Mathematica (Almagest) (c. 150 CE)

Claudius Ptolemy — Book I, Chapter 10. G. J. Toomer (trans.), Ptolemy’s Almagest, Duckworth / Springer, 1984. Cataloged in: GC-05.01

Letter to Michael Mästlin (Tübingen, October 1597) (1597)

Johannes Kepler — Gesammelte Werke, Vol. 13, Nr. 75. First explicit derivation of the right triangle with sides in geometric progression 1 : √φ : φ. Cataloged in: GC-04.02

Rational Recreations (1794)

William Hooper — 4th ed., Vol. 1, Problem XLIII. Early geometric missing-area rectangle dissection later generalized by Sam Loyd, Martin Gardner, and Paul Curry. Cataloged in: GC-07.06

Géométrographie ou Art des constructions géométriques (1902)

Émile Lemoine — Scientia No. 18, C. Naud, Paris. Foundational treatise quantifying operational simplicity of straightedge-and-compass constructions; see also J. Reusch, Planimetrische Konstruktionen in geometrographischer Ausführung (1904). Cataloged in: GC-01.03

The Divine Proportion: A Study in Mathematical Beauty (1970)

H. E. Huntley — Dover Publications, New York. Classic exposition including the angle-bisector circle division in the 3-4-5 right triangle. Cataloged in: GC-01.01, GC-01.02, GC-04.01

Elementary Problem E3007 (1983)

George Odom & H. S. M. Coxeter — American Mathematical Monthly 90(7), p. 482 (solution 93(7), 1986, p. 566). Equilateral-triangle midline extended to the circumcircle yields the golden section. Cataloged in: GC-02.01

A Simple Construction of the Golden Section (2002)

Kurt Hofstetter — Forum Geometricorum 2, pp. 65–66. Five-circle Mascheroni compass-only construction of the golden section. Cataloged in: GC-01.05

5-Step Division of a Segment in the Golden Section (2003)

Kurt Hofstetter — Forum Geometricorum 3, pp. 205–206. Minimal 5-step straightedge-and-compass internal division of a given segment. Cataloged in: GC-01.03

Another 5-Step Division of a Segment in the Golden Section (2004)

Kurt Hofstetter — Forum Geometricorum 4, pp. 21–22. Alternative 5-step straightedge-and-compass division using three circles and two lines. Cataloged in: GC-01.04

Division of a Segment in the Golden Section with Ruler and Rusty Compass (2005)

Kurt Hofstetter — Forum Geometricorum 5, pp. 135–136. Fixed-opening (rusty) compass construction dividing a unit segment in φ. Cataloged in: GC-01.06

Problems in Plane Geometry (2006)

V. V. Prasolov — MCCME, Moscow (5th ed.). Affine equi-area characterization of golden diagonal ratios in irregular convex pentagons. Cataloged in: GC-05.03

Golden Window (2010)

Jerzy Kocik — Mathematics Magazine 83(5), pp. 384–390. Six-circle Apollonian window packing forcing powers of φ, golden rectangles, and Kepler’s triangle. Cataloged in: GC-06.05

Another Simple Construction of the Golden Section (2011)

Michel Bataille — Forum Geometricorum 11, pp. 55–56. Constructs φ from an equilateral triangle and square erected on the same segment. Cataloged in: GC-02.02

Fibonacci Numbers and the Golden Section (1996–2024)

Ron Knott — University of Surrey Mathematical Archive. Companion archive cataloged in sibling GEOMETOR repository geometor/knott. URL: https://r-knott.surrey.ac.uk/Fibonacci/
