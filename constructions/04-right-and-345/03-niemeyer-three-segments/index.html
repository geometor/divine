GC-04.03 · Jo Niemeyer’s Three Equal Leaning Segments¶

Discoverer(s): Jo Niemeyer | Date: 2011 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \phi)\) | Loci: 3 (Lemoine \(S = 9\))

Place segment A1B1 perpendicular to line L at A1, lean equal segment B2A2 from midpoint B2 of A1B1 onto L, and lean a third equal segment B3A3 from midpoint B3 of B2A2 onto L. Then A2 divides A1A3 in the golden ratio: \(|A1A2|/|A2A3| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Segment A1B1 of length \(2\) from A1 \(= (0,0)\) to B1 \(= (2,0)\) perpendicular to vertical line L (\(x = 0\)).

step Bisect A1B1 at B2 \(= (1,0)\) and swing circle cB2 of radius \(2\) to meet L at A2 \(= (0, \sqrt{3})\).
step Bisect B2A2 at B3 \(= (\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})\).
step Swing circle cB3 of radius \(2\) centered at B3 to meet L above A2 at A3.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

Right triangle \(\triangle A1B2A2\) has hypotenuse \(2\) and leg \(1\), so \(|A1A2| = \sqrt{3}\) and B3 \(= (\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})\). Circle cB3 satisfies \((x - \tfrac{1}{2})^2 + (y - \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})^2 = 4\); at \(x = 0\), \((y - \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})^2 = \frac{15}{4}\), giving \(y_{A3} = \frac{\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{15}}{2} = \sqrt{3}\,\phi\). Hence \(|A1A2| = \sqrt{3}\) and \(|A2A3| = \sqrt{3}(\phi - 1) = \sqrt{3}/\phi\), proving \(|A1A2|/|A2A3| = \phi\).
