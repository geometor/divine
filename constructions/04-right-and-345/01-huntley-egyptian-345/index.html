GC-04.01 · H. E. Huntley’s Golden Ratio in the 3-4-5 Right Triangle (1970)¶

Discoverer(s): H. E. Huntley, Tran Quang Hung | Date: 1970 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\) | Loci: 2 (Lemoine \(S = 6\))

In a \(3\text{-}4\text{-}5\) right triangle ABC (\(|BC|=3, |AC|=4, |AB|=5\)), let the internal bisector of \(\angle ABC\) meet leg AC at O. Circle cOC centered at O through C intersects line BO at P and Q such that \(|PQ|/|BP| = |BQ|/|PQ| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Right triangle ABC with right angle at C \(= (0,0)\), B \(= (3,0)\), and A \(= (0,4)\).

step Bisect \(\angle ABC\) to meet leg AC at O \(= (0, \tfrac{3}{2})\).
step Draw circle cOC of radius \(\tfrac{3}{2}\) centered at O through C (tangent to both BC at C and hypotenuse AB at T).
step Extend bisector BO across cOC to obtain secant intersections P and Q.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

By the angle-bisector theorem, \(|CO|/|OA| = |BC|/|AB| = 3/5\), giving \(|CO| = 3/2\) and diameter \(|PQ| = 3 = |BC|\). By Euclid III.36 (tangent-secant power from B to cOC), \(|BC|^2 = |BP| \cdot |BQ| = |BP|(|BP| + |PQ|)\). Substituting \(|BC| = |PQ|\) gives \(|PQ|^2 = |BP|^2 + |BP|\cdot|PQ|\), whence \(|PQ|/|BP| = \phi\).
