GC-05.05 · Tran Quang Hung’s Six-Pentagon Reflection Star¶

Discoverer(s): Tran Quang Hung | Date: 2016 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi, \sqrt{10 - 2\sqrt{5}})\) | Loci: 3 (Lemoine \(S = 9\))

Reflect regular pentagon ABCDE across each of its five sides to obtain five surrounding congruent pentagons. Joining the outer lateral vertices across two intervening pentagons (such as chord S1) cuts every side of ABCDE in the golden ratio, dividing AB at G and H in a 4-point golden chain.

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Central regular pentagon ABCDE of side \(2\) on base A \(= (-1,0)\), B \(= (1,0)\).

step Reflect ABCDE across each of its five sides AB, BC, CD, DE, EA.
step Join the outer lateral vertices in the two chiral \(\{5/2\}\) star polygons (including chord S1).
step Mark intersections G and H where the star chords cross side AB.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

By fivefold dihedral symmetry \(D_5\), the two chiral \(\{5/2\}\) star polygons on the reflected lateral vertices meet side AB at mirror-symmetric points G and H. Using exact reflection across unit-quadrance-scaled sides, chord S1 meets \(y = 0\) at \(x = \pm(\sqrt{5} - 2)\), partitioning \([-1, 1]\) into segments of lengths \(2/\phi^2, 2/\phi^3, 2/\phi^2\) and proving \(|AG|/|GB| = \phi^{\pm 1}\).
