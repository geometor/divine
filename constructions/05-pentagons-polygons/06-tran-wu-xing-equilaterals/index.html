GC-05.06 · Tran Quang Hung’s Fivefold Wu Xing Equilateral Star¶

Discoverer(s): Tran Quang Hung | Date: 2015 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \phi, \sqrt{10 - 2\sqrt{5}})\) | Loci: 2 (Lemoine \(S = 6\))

Arrange five congruent equilateral triangles OV1W1 … OV5W5 of unit side with fivefold rotational symmetry around shared vertex O. The pentagram star joining outer vertices V1 … V5 cuts the radial sides of the equilateral triangles in the golden ratio, e.g. chord V2V5 cuts OW1 at F with \(|OF|/|FW1| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Center O \(= (0,0)\) and leading apex V1 \(= (0,1)\) of five \(72^\circ\)-spaced unit equilateral triangles.

step Construct the five \(72^\circ\)-rotated unit equilateral triangles OV1W1 … OV5W5 sharing vertex O.
step Draw pentagram chord V2V5 (along with the remaining four chords of star \(\{5/2\}\)).
step Mark intersection F of horizontal pentagram chord V2V5 with equilateral side OW1.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

Vertices V2 and V5 lie at angles \(90^\circ \pm 72^\circ\), so chord V2V5 is horizontal at height \(y = \sin 18^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{4} = \frac{1}{2\phi}\). Meanwhile, rotating V1 \(= (0,1)\) clockwise by \(60^\circ\) places W1 at \((\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \tfrac{1}{2})\) (\(30^\circ\) above the horizontal). Hence OW1 reaches height \(y_F = \frac{1}{2\phi}\) at distance \(|OF| = \frac{y_F}{\sin 30^\circ} = \frac{1}{\phi}\), leaving \(|FW1| = 1 - \frac{1}{\phi} = \frac{1}{\phi^2}\) and proving \(|OF|/|FW1| = \phi\).
