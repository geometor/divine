GC-05.04 · Tran Quang Hung’s Shared-Base Regular Pentagon & Hexagon¶

Discoverer(s): Tran Quang Hung | Date: 2015 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \phi, \sqrt{10 - 2\sqrt{5}})\) | Loci: 4 (Lemoine \(S = 12\))

Erect regular hexagon ABCDEF and regular pentagon AMNPF on the same side AF. The perpendiculars MQ and PR to diagonal MP at M and P intersect upper hexagon sides BC and DE at Q and R such that \(|CQ|/|QB| = |DR|/|RE| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Shared horizontal base A \(= (-1,0)\), F \(= (1,0)\) of length \(2\).

step Construct regular hexagon ABCDEF of side \(2\) above AF, with upper-left side B \(= (-2, \sqrt{3})\) to C \(= (-1, 2\sqrt{3})\).
step Construct regular pentagon AMNPF of side \(2\) on the same side of AF, with horizontal diagonal MP.
step Erect perpendiculars MQ and PR to MP at M and P, cutting BC at Q and DE at R.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

For base \(|AF| = 2\), diagonal MP of regular pentagon AMNPF has length \(2\phi\), so M has abscissa \(x_M = -\phi\) while B and C have abscissas \(x_B = -2\) and \(x_C = -1\). Because MQ is vertical (\(x = -\phi\)), linear projection onto the \(x\)-axis yields \(|CQ|/|QB| = (-1 - (-\phi))/(-\phi - (-2)) = (\phi - 1)/(2 - \phi) = (1/\phi)/(1/\phi^2) = \phi\).
