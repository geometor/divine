GC-06 · Circles, Apollonian Windows, Yin–Yang & Sangaku¶

Infinite rational secants, tangent equal circles, Kocik’s Golden Window, Yin–Yang, and Sangaku packings

Tangent circle packings translate tangency constraints into quadratic Descartes–Pythagoras equations whose positive roots repeatedly evaluate to \(\phi\). This family catalogs Bùi Quang Tuân’s infinite Diophantine secant pencil and four-circle vesica chain, tangent circle theorems by Elliot McGucken and Thanos Kalogerakis, Jerzy Kocik’s six-circle Apollonian Golden Window, John Arioni’s Yin–Yang secant, and three equal circles inscribed in a semicircle.
