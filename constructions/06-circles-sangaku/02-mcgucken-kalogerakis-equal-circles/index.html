GC-06.02 · Elliot McGucken & Thanos Kalogerakis’s Equal Tangent Circles¶

Discoverer(s): Elliot McGucken, Thanos Kalogerakis | Date: 2016 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\) | Loci: 4 (Lemoine \(S = 12\))

Let equal unit circles cB and cD touch externally at C, and let AF be the diameter of cB perpendicular to line of centers BD. Secant FD cuts cD at H and I such that \(|HI|/|FH| = |FI|/|HI| = |FI|/|AF| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Externally tangent unit circles cB at B \(= (0,0)\) and cD at D \(= (2,0)\), touching at C \(= (1,0)\).

step Erect perpendicular diameter AF of cB with F \(= (0,1)\) and A \(= (0,-1)\).
step Draw secant line FD through center D of circle cD.
step Mark intersections H (near) and I (far) of FD with cD.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

Right triangle \(\triangle FBD\) has legs \(|BF| = 1\) and \(|BD| = 2\), giving \(|FD| = \sqrt{5}\). Subtracting and adding radius \(1\) of cD yields \(|FH| = \sqrt{5} - 1 = 2/\phi\), diameter \(|HI| = 2\), and \(|FI| = \sqrt{5} + 1 = 2\phi\). Hence \(|HI|/|FH| = \phi\) (McGucken) and \(|FI|/|AF| = \phi\) (Kalogerakis).

\(Q(H, I) = 4\), \(Q(F, H) = 6 - 2 \sqrt{5}\), \(Q_{\text{long}}/Q_{\text{short}} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2} + \frac{3}{2} = \phi^2\)
