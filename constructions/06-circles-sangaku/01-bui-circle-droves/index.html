GC-06.01 · Bùi Quang Tuân’s Infinite Family of Golden Circle Secants¶

Discoverer(s): Bùi Quang Tuân | Date: 2014 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \phi)\) | Loci: 4 (Lemoine \(S = 12\))

In unit circle cO on diameter AB, infinitely many rational-abscissa point pairs on opposite semicircles—including C1 \(= (0,1)\), D1 \(= (\tfrac{2}{3}, -\tfrac{\sqrt{5}}{3})\) and C2 \(= (-\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})\), D2 \(= (\tfrac{7}{8}, -\tfrac{\sqrt{15}}{8})\)—concur on radius OB at the exact golden point M \(= (1/\phi^2, 0)\) where \(|MB|/|OM| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Unit circle cO centered at O \(= (0,0)\) with diameter A \(= (-1,0)\) to B \(= (1,0)\).

step Plot upper apex C1 \(= (0,1)\) and lower chord point D1 at abscissa \(x = \tfrac{2}{3}\) on cO.
step Plot a second rational-abscissa pair C2 at \(x = -\tfrac{1}{2}\) and D2 at \(x = \tfrac{7}{8}\) on opposite semicircles.
step Draw chords C1D1 and C2D2, which concur on OB at M \(= (\tfrac{3-\sqrt{5}}{2}, 0)\).

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

For \(C(x, \sqrt{1-x^2})\) and \(D(y, -\sqrt{1-y^2})\), chord \(CD\) crosses \(AB\) at \(x_M = \frac{x\sqrt{1-y^2} + y\sqrt{1-x^2}}{\sqrt{1-x^2} + \sqrt{1-y^2}}\). Setting \(x_M = \frac{3 - \sqrt{5}}{2} = \frac{1}{\phi^2}\) yields Bùi Quang Tuân’s Diophantine condition \(\frac{5(1 - x^2)}{1 - y^2} = \left(\frac{3 - 2y}{1 - 2x}\right)^2\), satisfied simultaneously by \((0, \tfrac{2}{3})\), \((-\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{7}{8})\), and infinitely many rational pairs.
