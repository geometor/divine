GC-06.06 · John Arioni’s Golden Secant in the Yin-Yang Circle¶

Discoverer(s): John Arioni | Date: 2016 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\) | Loci: 4 (Lemoine \(S = 12\))

Inscribe twin tangent circles cA and cC of radius \(\tfrac{1}{2}\) along horizontal diameter PLPR of unit circle cO, and let B be the apex of perpendicular radius OB. Secant BA cuts cA at H and I such that \(|HI|/|BH| = |BI|/|HI| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Unit circle cO centered at O \(= (0,0)\) with apex B \(= (0,1)\).

step Bisect radii OPL and OPR at A \(= (-\tfrac{1}{2}, 0)\) and C \(= (\tfrac{1}{2}, 0)\) and draw inner circles cA and cC through O.
step Join apex B to center A of circle cA.
step Mark intersections H and I of secant BA with cA.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

Right triangle \(\triangle AOB\) has legs \(|OA| = 1/2\) and \(|OB| = 1\), so \(|BA| = \sqrt{5}/2\). Since circle cA has radius \(1/2\) and diameter \(|HI| = 1\), we obtain \(|BH| = \frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2} = 1/\phi\) and \(|BI| = \frac{\sqrt{5}+1}{2} = \phi\), proving \(|HI|/|BH| = |BI|/|HI| = \phi\).
