GC-06.04 · Tran Quang Hung & Elliot McGucken’s Three Tangent Circles¶

Discoverer(s): Tran Quang Hung, Elliot McGucken | Date: 2016 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \phi)\) | Loci: 5 (Lemoine \(S = 15\))

Let congruent unit circles cA, cB, cC be mutually externally tangent at D, E, F, and let circle cO pass through their centers A, B, C. Line DF intersects cO at J and G to form a 4-point golden chain \(|DF|/|FG| = |DG|/|DF| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Equilateral center triangle ABC of side \(2\) centered at O \(= (0,0)\) with A \(= (0, \tfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3})\).

step Draw mutually tangent unit circles cA, cB, cC touching at midpoints D, E, F of ABC.
step Draw circumcircle cO of radius \(\tfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}\) through centers A, B, C.
step Extend midline DF across cO to obtain intersections G and J.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

Because D, E, F are the midpoints of equilateral ABC inscribed in cO, chord JG along midline DF reproduces George Odom’s configuration (GC-02.01 · George Odom’s Equilateral Midline & Circumcircle Chord (1983)) inside the three-circle packing: \(|DF| = 1\) and \(|FG| = |DJ| = \frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2} = 1/\phi\), hence \(|DF|/|FG| = \phi\).
