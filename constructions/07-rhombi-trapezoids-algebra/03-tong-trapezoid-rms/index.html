GC-07.03 · Jingcheng Tong’s Area-Bisecting Chord in a 1:3 Trapezoid¶

Discoverer(s): Jingcheng Tong | Date: 2005 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\) | Loci: 3 (Lemoine \(S = 9\))

Let ABCD be a trapezoid with parallel bases AD and BC satisfying \(|BC| = 3|AD|\). The chord MN parallel to the bases that bisects the area of ABCD divides both non-parallel legs AB and DC in the golden ratio: \(|AM|/|MB| = |DN|/|NC| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Trapezoid ABCD with top base A \(= (-1,2)\) to D \(= (1,2)\) (\(a = 2\)) and bottom base B \(= (-3,0)\) to C \(= (3,0)\) (\(b = 6\)).

step Construct trapezoid ABCD with parallel bases of lengths \(a = 2\) and \(b = 6\).
step Compute the root-mean-square chord length \(c = \sqrt{(a^2 + b^2)/2} = 2\sqrt{5}\).
step Draw parallel chord MN at height \(y = 3 - \sqrt{5}\) where horizontal width equals \(2\sqrt{5}\).

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

Any parallel chord of length \(c\) divides legs AB and DC in the ratio \(|AM|/|MB| = (c - a)/(b - c)\), and bisects the trapezoid’s area precisely when \(c^2 = (a^2 + b^2)/2\). For \(b = 3a\), \(c = \sqrt{5}\,a\), giving \(|AM|/|MB| = (\sqrt{5} - 1)/(3 - \sqrt{5}) = \phi\)—independent of leg slopes or height!

Historical Commentary & Attribution¶

Because area bisection depends only on the base ratio \(b/a = 3\), this theorem holds for every trapezoid (isosceles or scalene) whose parallel bases stand in ratio \(1 : 3\).
