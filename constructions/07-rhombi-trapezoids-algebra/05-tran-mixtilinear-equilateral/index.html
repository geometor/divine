GC-07.05 · Tran Quang Hung’s Mixtilinear Circle in an Equilateral Triangle¶

Discoverer(s): Tran Quang Hung, Alexander Bogomolny, Leonard Giugiuc | Date: 2016 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \phi)\) | Loci: 3 (Lemoine \(S = 9\))

In equilateral triangle ABC with circumcenter K and circumcircle cK, inscribe the A-mixtilinear circle cF tangent to sides AB, AC and internally to cK. The line through K parallel to AB meets BC at O and cF at I and J such that \(|KO|/|OJ| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Equilateral triangle ABC of side \(18\) with A \(= (-9,0)\), B \(= (9,0)\), C \(= (0, 9\sqrt{3})\), and circumcenter K \(= (0, 3\sqrt{3})\).

step Draw circumcircle cK of radius \(6\sqrt{3}\) around ABC.
step Inscribe A-mixtilinear circle cF centered at F \(= (3, 4\sqrt{3})\) of radius \(4\sqrt{3}\), tangent to AB at D \(= (3,0)\).
step Draw horizontal line KO (\(y = 3\sqrt{3}\)), cutting BC at O \(= (6, 3\sqrt{3})\) and cF at I and J.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

For side \(s = 18\), the A-mixtilinear inradius is \(r_A = \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{9}s = 4\sqrt{3}\) with \(|AD| = \frac{2}{3}s = 12\), placing F at \((3, 4\sqrt{3})\). Along \(y = 3\sqrt{3}\), \((x - 3)^2 + (3\sqrt{3} - 4\sqrt{3})^2 = 48\) yields \((x - 3)^2 = 45\), so \(x_J = 3 + 3\sqrt{5}\). Since \(x_K = 0\) and \(x_O = 6\), \(|KO| = 6\) and \(|OJ| = 3(\sqrt{5} - 1) = 6/\phi\), proving \(|KO|/|OJ| = \phi\).
