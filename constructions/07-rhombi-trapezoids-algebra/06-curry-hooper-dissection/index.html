GC-07.06 · Exact φ-Limit Resolution of the Hooper–Curry Fibonacci Dissection¶

Discoverer(s): William Hooper, Paul Curry, Alexander Bogomolny | Date: 1794 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\) | Loci: 1 (Lemoine \(S = 3\))

Replace integer Fibonacci lengths \((3, 5, 8, 13)\) in the \(8 \times 8 = 64\) vs. \(5 \times 13 = 65\) dissection fallacy by exact powers \((1, \phi, \phi^2, \phi^3)\). In rectangle ABCD of width \(\phi^3\) and height \(\phi\), internal vertices Q \(= (\phi, 1/\phi)\) and P \(= (\phi^2, 1)\) lie identically on diagonal AC, forming the 4-point golden chain \(|AQ|/|QP| = |AP|/|AQ| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Rectangle ABCD with A \(= (0,0)\), B \(= (\phi^3, 0)\), C \(= (\phi^3, \phi)\), and D \(= (0, \phi)\).

step Mark golden cuts E \(= (\phi^2, 0)\) on AB and F \(= (\phi, \phi)\) on DC.
step Erect vertical cuts EP of height \(1\) to P \(= (\phi^2, 1)\) and FQ of length \(1\) to Q \(= (\phi, 1/\phi)\).
step Draw diagonal AC, which passes with zero area gap through both Q and P.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

For Fibonacci numbers, Cassini’s identity \(F_{n-1}F_{n+1} - F_n^2 = (-1)^n\) produces a thin parallelogram of area \(\pm 1\) because slopes \(F_{n-2}/F_n\) and \(F_{n-1}/F_{n+1}\) differ slightly. Replacing \(F_k\) by \(\phi^k\) gives \(\phi \cdot \phi^3 - (\phi^2)^2 = 0\) and identical slopes \(\text{slope}(A,Q) = (1/\phi)/\phi = 1/\phi^2 = \text{slope}(A,P) = \text{slope}(A,C)\). Along AC, abscissas \((0, \phi, \phi^2, \phi^3)\) yield \(|AQ|/|QP| = \phi / 1 = \phi\).
