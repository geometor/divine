GC-02.07 · Butterfly Chords Astride an Equilateral Triangle¶

Discoverer(s): Nguyen Thanh Dung | Date: 2016 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \phi)\) | Loci: 6 (Lemoine \(S = 18\))

Let I be the midpoint of radius OJ of circle cO, XT the chord perpendicular to OJ through I, and ICD an equilateral triangle symmetric about OJ with C and D on cO. If rays CI and DI meet cO again at A and B, then chords BC and AD cut XT at Y and Z with \(|YZ|/|ZT| = |YT|/|YZ| = |YZ|/|XY| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Circle cO centered at O \(= (0, 0)\) through J \(= (0, 2)\) with radius midpoint I \(= (0, 1)\).

step Draw chord XT through I perpendicular to OJ, meeting cO at X \(= (-\sqrt{3}, 1)\) and T \(= (\sqrt{3}, 1)\).
step Inscribe equilateral triangle ICD with apex I and base vertices C, D on cO.
step Extend CI and DI to meet cO at A and B, then draw butterfly chords BC and AD cutting XT at Y and Z.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

For C and D on \(x^2 + y^2 = 4\) forming equilateral ICD of side \(s\) below I \(= (0, 1)\), \((s/2)^2 + (1 - s\sqrt{3}/2)^2 = 4 \implies s^2 - \sqrt{3}\,s - 3 = 0\), giving \(s = \sqrt{3}\,\phi\) directly. By the Butterfly Theorem and symmetry across OJ, \(|IY| = |IZ| = \sqrt{3}/(2\phi)\), while \(|IT| = \sqrt{3}\). Hence \(|YZ| = \sqrt{3}/\phi\) and \(|ZT| = \sqrt{3}(1 - 1/(2\phi))\)—moreover \(|YZ|/|ZT| = |YT|/|YZ| = |YZ|/|XY| = \phi\), forming the 4-point golden chain \((X, Y, Z, T)\).
