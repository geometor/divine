GC-02.06 · Regular Hexagon & External Square Compass Sweep¶

Discoverer(s): Tran Quang Hung | Date: 2014 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \phi)\) | Loci: 3 (Lemoine \(S = 9\))

Given regular hexagon ABCDEF and external square ABHG on side AB, circle cAH centered at A through opposite square corner H cuts hexagon sides EF at I and BC at L in the golden ratio: \(|FI|/|IE| = |BL|/|LC| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Regular hexagon ABCDEF on side AB from A \(= (0, 0)\) to B \(= (2, 0)\).

step Construct regular hexagon ABCDEF of side \(2\) above AB.
step Construct external square ABHG of side \(2\) below AB and diagonal AH of length \(2\sqrt{2}\).
step Draw circle cAH centered at A through H, intersecting side EF at I and side BC at L.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

In \(\triangle AFI\), \(|AF| = 2\), \(\angle AFI = 120^\circ\), and radius \(|AI| = |AH| = 2\sqrt{2}\). Letting \(t = |FI|\), the Law of Cosines gives \(8 = 4 + t^2 - 2(2)t\cos 120^\circ = t^2 + 2t + 4\), so \(t^2 + 2t - 4 = 0\) and \(t = \sqrt{5} - 1 = 2/\phi\). Since side \(|FE| = 2\), the remaining segment is \(|IE| = 2 - (\sqrt{5} - 1) = 3 - \sqrt{5} = 2/\phi^2\), proving \(|FI|/|IE| = \phi\).

Historical Commentary & Attribution¶

Notice the structural duality between Tran Quang Hung’s hexagon construction (Cut-the-Knot) and Michel Bataille’s equilateral triangle construction: replacing the \(60^\circ\) interior angle with the hexagon’s \(120^\circ\) angle flips \(t^2 - 2t - 4 = 0\) to \(t^2 + 2t - 4 = 0\), bringing the intersection inside side EF.
