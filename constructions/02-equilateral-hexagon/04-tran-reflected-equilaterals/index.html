GC-02.04 · Tran Quang Hung’s Reflected Equilateral Triangles¶

Discoverer(s): Tran Quang Hung | Date: 2014 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \phi)\) | Loci: 4 (Lemoine \(S = 12\))

Reflect equilateral triangle ABC across base AB to ABC1 with circumcircle cO1. If midpoints M of BC and N of AB are joined to meet cO1 at P, then line AP divides side BC at D in the golden ratio \(|CD|/|DB| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Shared base AB from A \(= (-1, 0)\) to B \(= (1, 0)\) with reflected equilateral apices C \(= (0, \sqrt{3})\) and C1 \(= (0, -\sqrt{3})\).

step Construct equilateral triangles ABC and ABC1, and circumcircle cO1 of ABC1 centered at O1 \(= (0, -\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3})\).
step Join midpoint M \(= (\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})\) of BC and midpoint N \(= (0, 0)\) of AB, extending ray MN to meet cO1 at P.
step Draw line AP to intersect side BC at D.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

Line MN passes through the origin with slope \(\sqrt{3}\) (\(y = \sqrt{3}\,x\)). Intersecting with cO1 \((x^2 + (y + \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3})^2 = \tfrac{4}{3})\) gives \(4x^2 + 2x - 1 = 0\), whose positive root is \(x_P = \frac{\sqrt{5} - 1}{4} = \frac{1}{2\phi}\) and \(y_P = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2\phi}\), so \(|NP| = 1/\phi\) along NM (of length \(1\)). Because MN is the midline parallel to AC and N bisects AB, projecting P from A onto BC yields \(|CD|/|DB| = \phi\).

Invariant

Exact Symbolic Value

CD \(/\) DB \(= \frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}\) on collinear triple (C, D, B)

\(|CD| = -1 + \sqrt{5}\), \(|DB| = 3 - \sqrt{5}\)

Rational Trigonometry Quadrances

\(Q(C, D) = 6 - 2 \sqrt{5}\), \(Q(D, B) = 14 - 6 \sqrt{5}\), \(Q_{\text{long}}/Q_{\text{short}} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2} + \frac{3}{2} = \phi^2\)

Algebraic Number Field

\(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \phi)\)
