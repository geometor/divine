GC-02.05 · Nested Equilateral Triangles on the Incircle¶

Discoverer(s): Tran Quang Hung | Date: 2014 | Field: \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \phi)\) | Loci: 4 (Lemoine \(S = 12\))

Inscribe equilateral triangle FGH in the incircle cIn of equilateral triangle ABC so that extended sides HF, FG, GH pass through vertices A, C, B respectively. Then \(|FH|/|AF| = |FG|/|CF_1|\) divides each secant in the golden ratio \(|FH|/|AF| = \phi\).

Given & Constructive Steps¶

Given: Equilateral triangle ABC of circumradius \(2\) centered at O \(= (0, 0)\) with unit incircle cIn.

step Construct equilateral triangle ABC and its unit incircle cIn centered at O.
step Inscribe equilateral triangle FGH of side \(\sqrt{3}\) in cIn such that F lies on segment AH, G on CF, and H on BG.
step Draw the three three-fold symmetric secants AH, BG, and CF.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

For vertex A \(= (0, 2)\) and unit circle cIn, the power of A with respect to cIn is \(|OA|^2 - 1^2 = 4 - 1 = 3\). Along secant AFH, any equilateral triangle FGH inscribed in cIn has side \(|FH| = \sqrt{3}\). By the Tangent-Secant / Power-of-a-Point theorem, \(|AF| \cdot |AH| = |AF|(|AF| + \sqrt{3}) = 3\). Dividing by \(3\) gives \((|AF|/\sqrt{3})^2 + (|AF|/\sqrt{3}) - 1 = 0\), so \(|AF| = \sqrt{3}/\phi\) and \(|FH|/|AF| = \phi\).
