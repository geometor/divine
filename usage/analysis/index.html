analysis¶

The core functionality of divine is to find patterns.

golden sections

Divine identifies points that divide segments into the golden ratio (\(\phi\)).

harmonic ranges

Divine identifies harmonic ranges, which are sets of four collinear points in a specific cross-ratio relationship.

pattern recognition

Divine groups related elements into chains and families to reveal hidden structures.
